# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Read the [documentation](https://saquibpm.github.io/faun-design-system), then ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/SaquibPM/faun-design-system/discussions).
- **Found a bug?** Open an [issue](https://github.com/SaquibPM/faun-design-system/issues/new/choose) with a minimal reproduction.
- **Want a new component or token?** Open a component proposal or feature request.
- **Security issue?** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
