## When to use

- To preview an agent, run, person or evidence item that a link points to, so people can decide whether to follow it.
- When the preview is read-only and the full detail lives at the link.

## When not to use

- For a label or hint on a control. Use [Tooltip](/components/tooltip).
- For content with actions, forms or anything people must reach. Use [Popover](/components/popover).
- On touch-first screens as the only route to information. Touch devices have no hover.

## Anatomy

A raised panel with a subtle border and elevation 3, opening beside its trigger with a short delay. It uses the popover radius and padding.

## Sizes

Small is 240 wide and medium is 320. Cards never exceed the viewport width less a small margin.

## Notes

- `HoverCard` is the Radix root: it accepts `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `openDelay` (700ms) and `closeDelay` (300ms).
- Wrap a link or button with `HoverCardTrigger asChild` so the trigger is focusable.
