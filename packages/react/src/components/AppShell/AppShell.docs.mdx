## When to use

- Once, at the root of the product, around every signed-in page.
- `PageHeader` at the top of every page inside the shell, so titles, status and the primary action always sit in the same place.

## When not to use

- Marketing pages, sign-in and onboarding flows: they have no navigation to show.
- Secondary navigation inside a page: use Tabs. Steps of a flow: use Stepper.
- Embedded or print views of a report: render the page content without the shell.

## Side navigation

The open panel has one primary action ("New conversation") under the workspace, then a few short sections (Projects, Reports, Pinned, Recent). Rows are plain text; a section gives the collapsed rail its icon. Reports rows show progress as a percentage with a thin bar, pinned rows carry a small dot, and any row can have a kebab menu. A neutral count helps people choose; an attention badge appears only when something is waiting on the person signed in.

## Collapsed rail

People can collapse the navigation to a 64px near-black rail to give tables and graphs more room. It is the same design with fewer parts: the mark tile, a square moss "+" for the primary action, one icon per section (Projects, Reports, Pinned) that opens its rows in a panel beside the rail, and the round avatar at the bottom. Labels move into tooltips, counts and progress hide, and attention badges become a dot on the icon. The choice is the person's; persist it per user with `navCollapsed` and `onNavCollapsedChange`.

See the open and collapsed states side by side, with a working toggle, a keyboard shortcut and remembered state, in the [Assistant home](/patterns/assistant-home) pattern. [Chat with panels](/patterns/chat-with-panels) puts a conversation, a progress panel and an artifact panel beside the same navigation.

## Small screens

Below 768px the navigation becomes an off-canvas drawer opened from the menu button in the top bar. The page behind it becomes inert, Escape or choosing a destination closes it, and focus returns to the menu button. The top bar hides the search field; put a search action in `actions` if search must stay one tap away.

## Anatomy

1. **SideNav**: workspace block (mark tile and wordmark), the primary action, sections of rows and the signed-in user pinned to the bottom with a chevron. Open, it is a 280px light paper panel on `sidebar.bg` with a hairline border; in dark themes it takes a theme-appropriate dark surface. Collapsed, it is a 64px near-black rail on `rail.bg` in every theme. One component draws both, from the same mark, action, avatar and corner language. See ADR 0011.
2. **TopBar**: a 56px sticky bar on the surface colour with a hairline bottom border.
3. **Page area**: the sage canvas, padded by the page padding tokens (24/32/40px by breakpoint). Cards and KPI rows sit on it as white surfaces with a soft shadow.
4. **PageHeader**: `heading-xl` title, muted meta line with 14px icons, and actions aligned to the title.

## States

Items have rest, hover (`sidebar.bg-hover`, label to `sidebar.fg-active`), current (`sidebar.bg-active`, semibold label and a 3px `sidebar.accent` bar) and keyboard focus (a 2px ring in `sidebar.accent`, 3:1 on the surface and on both fills). Section labels, counts and context use `sidebar.fg-muted`; attention badges use `sidebar.badge-bg` and `sidebar.badge-fg`. The collapsed rail reads the matching `rail.*` tokens.

## Routing

Pass your router's link through `asChild`; the item keeps its icon, label, count and current state.

```tsx
<SideNavItem asChild label="Filings" icon={<IconFileText />} current={pathname.startsWith("/filings")} count={5}>
  <Link to="/filings" />
</SideNavItem>
```

## Panel parts

```tsx
<SideNav
  workspace={<SideNavWorkspace mark={<IconOrchestrator />} name="Faun" />}
  action={<SideNavAction>New conversation</SideNavAction>}
  footer={<SideNavUser name="Saquib Jawed" meta="Sustainability lead" onOpenMenu={openMenu} />}
>
  <SideNavSection title="Projects" icon={<IconFolder />} action={<IconButton icon={<IconAdd />} label="New project" size="sm" />}>
    <SideNavItem href="/acme" actions={<IconButton icon={<IconMoreVertical />} label="Actions for Acme" size="sm" />}>Acme</SideNavItem>
  </SideNavSection>
  <SideNavSection title="Reports" icon={<IconList />}>
    <SideNavItem href="/csrd" progress={72}>CSRD statement</SideNavItem>
  </SideNavSection>
  <SideNavSection title="Pinned" icon={<IconStar />}>
    <SideNavItem href="/ledger" dot>Emissions ledger</SideNavItem>
  </SideNavSection>
</SideNav>
```

A section with a `title` and an `icon` becomes one icon on the collapsed rail that opens its rows in a panel; sections without an icon (Recent) are left out of the rail. `progress` is 0 to 100 or `"done"`. `actions` renders beside the link, so the menu button is its own tab stop and never nests inside the link.

## Notes

- `fill="viewport"` (default) sizes the shell to the window and scrolls the page area. Use `fill="container"` to embed it, as these docs do.
- `useAppShell()` exposes `collapsed`, `mobile`, `mobileOpen` and their setters for custom controls; `AppShellNavToggle` renders the standard toggle anywhere inside the shell.
- The skip link is the [Skip link](/components/skip-link) component, aimed at the `<main>` element, which is focusable so the jump lands correctly.
- `SideNavGroup` takes `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange` and `current`. It detects `current` on its direct items. Second-tier `SideNavItem`s take no icon.
- `TopBar` takes `nav` (header links) before `start`. Put `HeaderActions` in `actions`.

## Screen readers

- A group is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at its list of pages; in the collapsed rail it is a button that opens a panel named by the group.
- The rail is a `nav` landmark named "Main"; the page is `main`. Sections are lists named by their titles.
- The current page has `aria-current="page"`. Counts are read after the label ("Filings 5"); attention badges are read as "Approvals 2 awaiting you".
- When collapsed, labels stay in the accessibility tree; only their visual text moves into a tooltip.
- The toggle reports `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. While the drawer is open the rest of the page is `inert`.

## Visual

- Item labels, section titles, counts and badge numbers meet 4.5:1 on `sidebar.bg` and on the hover and current fills in every theme (7:1 in the high-contrast themes). Icons, the current-item bar, the focus ring and the attention dot meet 3:1. These pairs are in the contrast contract.
- Items are 32px tall with full-width hit areas. The attention badge never relies on colour alone: it carries a number.
