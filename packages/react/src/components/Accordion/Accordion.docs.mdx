## When to use

- For supporting content most people don't need at once: methodology notes, advanced options, FAQs.
- To keep a long settings or review page scannable while keeping everything on one page.

## When not to use

- For the main content of the page. Don't hide what people came to see.
- For sections people compare or switch between often: use Tabs.
- For a single optional region: use Collapsible.

## Anatomy

A full-width trigger with optional icon, title, description, meta and a chevron that rotates when open, followed by the content. Divided items share hairlines; separated items are bordered cards.

## Notes

- `type="single"` needs `collapsible` for people to close the open item.
- Content height animates; reduced motion removes the animation.

## Screen readers

- Triggers are buttons inside headings, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Collapsed content is hidden from assistive technology.
- Disabled sections stay announced as unavailable.
