## When to use

- To show a person that an agent is working on a task they asked for in a conversation, and which step it is on.
- For a short list of steps, up to about eight, that a person can read at a glance.
- Docked beside the conversation for work that takes minutes, so it stays visible while the person keeps typing.

## When not to use

- To audit a run: tool calls, inputs, outputs, nested sub-agents. Use [Run trail](/components/run-trail).
- For one operation with a percentage and no steps. Use [Progress](/components/progress).
- For a decision waiting on a person. Use an approval card; the panel only says that a step is waiting.

## Writing

- The title is the task in the present participle: “Drafting the ESRS E1 disclosure”.
- Step titles start with a verb and fit on one line. Put counts and reasons in the detail line, not the title.
- Show elapsed time as given by your timer. Do not estimate time remaining unless you can stand behind it.

## Anatomy

A header with the title, the overall status badge, any actions and a Close button when docked. Under it one summary line (“Step 2 of 5: Apply emission factors · Elapsed 5 min 50 s”) and a progress bar. Below, a vertical list of steps, each with a marker, a title, its time, a status badge and an optional detail line.

## Status

Each step and the whole task use the `run` and `task` domains of the status map: tone, icon and word together, never colour alone. The current step is bold and carries `aria-current="step"`. The marker of a running step pulses; it stops under reduced motion.

## Notes

- The panel is controlled by data: pass `steps` and `elapsedMs` and rerender as the task advances. It owns only whether the list is folded (`open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`).
- The overall `state` is derived (failed, blocked, waiting for a person, completed, otherwise running). Pass `state` to override it.
- `variant="docked"` takes focus when it mounts and gives it back when it unmounts. Inline panels never take focus. Set `autoFocus` to change either.
- Inside a [Chat layout](/components/chat-layout) a docked panel sits above the artifact panel, or alone in the right column, and becomes a bottom sheet under 768 px.

## Screen readers

- The panel is a region named by its title. The steps are an ordered list named “Steps”, and the current one is marked `aria-current="step"`.
- Every status is a word as well as an icon: “Done”, “Running”, “Waiting for you”.
- A polite live region announces a change of current step (“Step 3 of 5, Draft the E1-6 narrative, in progress”). It says nothing when the panel first appears, and the progress bar announces its own milestones.
- The fold toggle is a button inside the heading with `aria-expanded`.

## Visual

- Status never relies on colour: the badge has an icon and a word. Markers are decoration.
- Targets meet the minimum size. Focus rings are visible on the toggle, the title and every button.
- Forced colours: markers use the text colour and the inline card keeps a visible border.
