## When to use

- When an agent must stop for a person before it files, publishes, spends or overwrites something.
- For one decision with a clear yes, no or “change this”. Put the evidence the person needs inside the card.

## When not to use

- For confirming a destructive click the person just made. Use [Alert dialog](/components/alert-dialog).
- For FYI messages that need no answer. Use [Banner](/components/banner) or [Toast](/components/toast).
- For many decisions in a queue. Show a table of requests and open one card per decision.

## Writing

- The title names the thing and the action: “Approve the ESRS E1-6 draft for FY2025”.
- The summary states what the agent did and the number that matters, in one or two sentences.
- Buttons stay as Approve, Request changes and Reject. The one solid accent button is Approve. Reject turns solid danger only in its confirmation step.

## Anatomy

A header with the approval gate icon, the title, who asked and when, and an approval status badge. A body with the summary, evidence, and what happens after approve or reject. A footer with actions. While a decision is pending a plum edge marks the card as waiting on a person.

## States

Pending shows actions. Approved and rejected show who decided, when and why, with no actions. While an opened form is showing, the footer holds a labelled text area with a confirm and a cancel.

## Notes

- `request` is an `ApprovalRequest`. Update its `state`, `decidedBy`, `decidedAt` and `comment` after you save the decision, and the card switches to its decided view.
- `onRequestChanges` leaves the request pending. Store the comment and let the agent revise.
- Pass `busy` while a decision is saving. Actions are disabled and Approve shows a spinner.
- Use `headingLevel` to fit the page outline.

## Screen readers

- The card is a region named by its title. The approval state is a word in a badge.
- Opening a reason form moves focus to its text area, which is labelled and required.
- Confirm stays disabled until there is a reason, so a decision without a reason cannot be sent.

## Visual

- Waiting on a person is shown by a plum edge, the approval icon and the words “Approval needed”, never by colour alone.
- Actions wrap to full-width buttons on narrow screens so targets stay large.
