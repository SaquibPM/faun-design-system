## When to use

- To start a run, ask a question or give an agent an instruction.
- Under a conversation with an agent, or at the foot of a panel that acts on the current filing.

## When not to use

- For a long form field such as a narrative. Use [Textarea](/components/textarea) in a [Field](/components/field).
- For a single search term. Use [Search field](/components/search-field).

## Writing

- Give the box a label that says who is listening: “Message to the CSRD Filing Orchestrator”. It is read by screen readers and need not be visible.
- Use the hint for what the agent can see or what Enter does, in one sentence.

<Section tab="style">

## Anatomy

One rounded surface that holds an attachments row, an auto-growing text box (one to eight lines), a toolbar with your actions on the start side, and Send or Stop on the end side. A hint sits below.

## States

Empty (Send off), ready, running (Stop), disabled. The whole surface shows the focus ring when the text box has focus.

## Notes

- Works controlled (`value` and `onValueChange`) or uncontrolled (`defaultValue`). An uncontrolled composer clears itself after `onSend`. A controlled one leaves clearing to you.
- `onSend` receives trimmed text. Send is off for empty text unless `allowEmpty` is set.
- Enter is ignored while an input method is composing, so people typing in Japanese or Hindi are never cut off.

## Screen readers

- The text box has an accessible name from `label`, and the hint is its description.
- Send and Stop are labelled buttons with a tooltip that shows the shortcut.

## Visual

- Controls meet the minimum target size. The surface and the buttons show visible focus.
