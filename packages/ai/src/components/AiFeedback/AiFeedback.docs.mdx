## When to use

- Under a completed AI response in a chat or beside a generated draft.
- Wherever the product will actually use the signal: rating feeds evaluation, regenerate reruns the agent.

## When not to use

- While the response is still streaming. Show Stop instead; [Streaming text](/components/streaming-text) does.
- For approving agent work that needs a decision. Use [Approval card](/components/approval-card).

## Writing

Names are short verbs and adjectives: Helpful, Not helpful, Copy, Regenerate. Confirmations are sentences: “Copied to the clipboard.”

A row of small icon buttons: thumbs up, thumbs down, copy, regenerate. A pressed rating is tinted in the AI colours and carries a pressed state. Reasons are small toggle chips.

## Notes

- `value` (`helpful`, `not-helpful`, `null`) and `onValueChange` control the rating; pressing the active rating clears it.
- `text` is copied with the Clipboard API; `onCopy` is called afterwards, and alone it shows Copy too.
- `hide` removes `rating`, `copy` or `regenerate`.

## Screen readers

- The controls are a group named “Response actions”. Ratings are toggle buttons with a pressed state.
- Outcomes are announced politely: “Marked as helpful.”, “Copied to the clipboard.”, “Thanks. Sent: Missing sources.”

## Visual

- Copy swaps its icon for a check and its name for “Copied”, so the change is not colour alone. Targets meet the minimum size.
