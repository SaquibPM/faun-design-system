## When to use

- For part-to-whole with many parts and a hierarchy: Scope 3 by category and source, spend by region and supplier.
- With `colorBy="change"`, to show size and direction of change together.

## When not to use

- For a few parts. Use a pie, donut or bar chart.
- To compare exact values. Use a bar chart; area is hard to compare.
- For values that can be negative.

Title with the whole and the period. Keep labels short: they sit on the tiles and are cut to fit.

## Anatomy

Title and description, a legend (groups) or colour key (size or change), the plot (group headings, tiles packed by the squarified algorithm, labels on chips for tiles big enough to hold them) and a tooltip with the path, value and share.

## Colour

`group`: top-level groups take `dataviz.cat-1` to `cat-8` in order. `value`: `dataviz.seq-1` to `seq-7`. `change`: the diverging ramp. Labels sit on a surface-coloured chip, so text contrast does not depend on the tile.

## Notes

- `data` is a tree of `{ label, value?, change?, children? }`. The root is only a container; its children are the top-level groups. Branches take the sum of their leaves.
- Leaves with no value, and empty branches, are dropped. Up to eight groups get their own colour.
- `positiveIs` (for `change`) says whether a positive change is the better or the worse end; default `worse`, for emissions.
- `toggleSeries` makes the legend interactive. Control it with `hiddenSeries` and `onHiddenSeriesChange` (ids of the hidden groups), or start from `defaultHiddenSeries`.
- The width follows the container; set `height` in px to change the plot height.
- No chart library is used. The chart is plain SVG, so it prints and themes with the tokens. Colours are always data-viz tokens; a test fails if any chart source contains a literal colour.

## Screen readers

- The chart is a figure named by its title. The drawing is hidden from assistive technology; a data table with every value, its unit and “No data” gaps takes its place.
- Moving with the arrow keys announces the item's path, value, share and change in a polite live region.
- The data table lists every leaf with its path, group, value and share, and the total at the foot.

## Visual

- Hovering shows a tooltip with the values for the mark under the pointer; keyboard focus shows the same tooltip.
- Tiles are separated by a surface-coloured gap, and the active tile gets a foreground outline.
- The sequential and diverging ramps are ordered by lightness, so shade can be read without hue.
