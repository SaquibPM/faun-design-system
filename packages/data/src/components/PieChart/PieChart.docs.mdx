## When to use

- To show the share of a few parts in one whole: emissions by scope, electricity by source, assurance status.
- Donut, when the total or a headline share should sit in the middle.

## When not to use

- For more than five or six parts. Group the tail as “Other”, or use a treemap or bar chart.
- To compare values between charts. Use a bar chart; angles are hard to compare.
- For parts that do not add up to a whole. Use a bar chart.

## Writing

Title with the whole and the period: “Emissions by scope, FY2026”. Say in the description which slice is biggest and by how much. Put the unit in `unit`.

## Anatomy

Title and description, a legend, the plot (one slice per part, optionally with its share written on it, and in a donut the total and a label in the hole) and a tooltip with value and share.

## Colour

Slices take `dataviz.cat-1` to `cat-8` in order. Slices with the id `scope-1`, `scope-2` or `scope-3` take the GHG scope colours. A hairline surface gap separates neighbours; the active slice gets a foreground outline.

## Notes

- `slices` is `{ id, label, value }[]`. Values must be zero or positive; a zero slice is not drawn but stays in the legend.
- At most eight slices are drawn; a warning is logged beyond that.
- `showShares` writes each share on slices wide enough to hold it; shares are always in the tooltip and table.
- `toggleSeries` makes the legend interactive. Control it with `hiddenSeries` and `onHiddenSeriesChange` (ids of the hidden series), or start from `defaultHiddenSeries`.
- The width follows the container; set `height` in px to change the plot height.
- No chart library is used. The chart is plain SVG, so it prints and themes with the tokens. Colours are always data-viz tokens; a test fails if any chart source contains a literal colour.

## Screen readers

- The chart is a figure named by its title. The drawing is hidden from assistive technology; a data table with every value, its unit and “No data” gaps takes its place.
- Moving with the arrow keys announces the slice's name, value and share in a polite live region.
- The data table lists every slice with its value and share, and the total at the foot.

## Visual

- Hovering shows a tooltip with the values for the mark under the pointer; keyboard focus shows the same tooltip.
- Marks against the surface meet 3:1 in every theme, and the categorical palette is checked for protan and deutan separation.
- Slice order is the order you pass, starting at twelve o'clock and running clockwise.
