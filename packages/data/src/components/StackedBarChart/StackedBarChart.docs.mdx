## When to use

- To show the parts of a total across a few categories or years: emissions by scope, Scope 3 by category.
- When the total matters as much as the split. The tooltip and data table include it.

## When not to use

- To compare the parts themselves across categories. Stacked segments share no baseline except the first; use a clustered bar chart.
- With mixed positive and negative values. They stack on opposite sides of the baseline, which is hard to read. Use a diverging bar chart.
- For more than five or six segments. Group the small ones.

## Writing

Order series so the largest, steadiest part is at the bottom, and keep that order across charts. Lead the title with the measure: “Gross emissions by scope, 2022 to 2026”.

## Colour

GHG scopes always use `dataviz.scope-1`, `scope-2` and `scope-3` (and `scope-outside` for outside-of-scopes). Other series take `cat-1` to `cat-8` in order.

## Anatomy

Segments have a hairline surface-coloured edge, so adjacent colours stay separate even when they are close in lightness. With `showTotals` the total is written above each stack when there is room.

## Notes

- Series order is stack order, bottom to top.
- Set `color` on a series to choose a slot explicitly. Do not reuse scope colours for something else.
- `references` draws target and reference lines at a total; `toggleSeries` with `hiddenSeries` / `onHiddenSeriesChange` (or `defaultHiddenSeries`) lets people hide segments. Totals count visible series only, and at least one series stays visible.
- The data table gains a Total column and the tooltip a total row.

## Screen readers

- A figure named by its title, with a data table fallback: one row per category, one column per series, and a total.
- Arrow keys announce the category, every part and the total.

- With `toggleSeries`, each legend entry is a button with `aria-pressed` (true = shown). Hiding or showing is announced politely, for example “Market-based hidden”. The last visible series is marked `aria-disabled` and stays on.
- Hidden series leave the drawing, the tooltip, the live announcements and the data table, so what is read matches what is seen.
- Target and reference lines are listed at the foot of the data table with their kind, label and value, for example “Target: 2030 pathway: 14,000 tCO₂e”.

## Visual

- Segment edges and the legend keep parts distinguishable; the scope colours are checked for colour-vision deficiency.
